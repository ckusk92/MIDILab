# Code of Conduct

MIDILab is intended to be a welcoming open-source project where people can discuss software and music-related ideas constructively.

## Expected behavior

Participants are expected to:

- Be respectful and constructive in issues, pull requests, reviews, and discussions.
- Critique ideas and code without attacking the person who contributed them.
- Assume good faith while still giving clear technical feedback.
- Welcome contributors with different experience levels and backgrounds.
- Keep discussions relevant to the project.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following are not acceptable:

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory or demeaning language.
- Sexualized comments or unwanted sexual attention.
- Deliberate disruption, spam, or repeated bad-faith behavior.
- Publishing private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject comments, issues, contributions, or other participation that violates these expectations. Repeated or serious violations may result in temporary or permanent restrictions from project spaces.

For sensitive conduct reports, contact the repository owner privately through a contact method published on their GitHub profile. Do not open a public issue containing private or sensitive information.
